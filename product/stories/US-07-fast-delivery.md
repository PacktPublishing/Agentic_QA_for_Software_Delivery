# US-07 Fast delivery

**As a** customer
**I want to** get my books quickly
**So that** I can start reading sooner

## Acceptance criteria

1. Delivery should be fast.
2. At checkout the customer can choose Express delivery, which costs a little more than
   standard shipping.
3. Orders placed late in the day are still delivered quickly with Express.
4. The order confirmation tells the customer when to expect the books.
5. Express delivery is available to all customers.
