# US-08 Free-shipping progress bar

**As a** customer
**I want to** see how close my cart is to free shipping
**So that** I can add another book and save on shipping

## Acceptance criteria

1. The cart page shows a progress bar towards free shipping.
2. While the cart subtotal is below $45.00, the bar says "Add $X more to get free shipping",
   where X is $45.00 minus the cart subtotal.
3. When the cart subtotal goes over $45.00, the bar is full and says
   "You've got free shipping!".
4. The progress bar is hidden when the cart is empty.
